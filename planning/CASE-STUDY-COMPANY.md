# Fictional Case-Study Company (DRAFT)

> All portfolio work uses this FICTIONAL company. Any resemblance to a real
> organization is coincidental. Label it as fictional everywhere it appears.

**Name:** Fernhill Payments, Inc. (fictional)
**What it does:** B2B SaaS platform for invoice payments and expense management
**Size:** ~120 employees, remote-first, US-based
**Tech:** Cloud-native on AWS; Google Workspace; Okta SSO; GitHub; Jira
**Data:** Customer financial data, PII; card payments handled by a third-party processor

## Why it needs GRC (the business driver)
Enterprise customers keep asking for a **SOC 2 Type II** report before signing.
Sales deals are stalling. Leadership wants a security program that is
"audit-ready in 12 months" without slowing engineering down.

## Framework scope
- **Primary:** SOC 2 (Trust Services Criteria: Security, Availability, Confidentiality)
- **Mapping reference:** NIST CSF 2.0 (as the organizing structure)
- **Secondary (later):** PCI DSS v4.0.1 scoping: how using a payment processor reduces scope
- **Out of scope for now:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022 (possible later mapping project)
