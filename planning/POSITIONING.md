# Positioning (DRAFT v0.1)

## Target role (primary)
Entry-level **GRC Analyst / IT Compliance Analyst**, with a technical edge
(automation, evidence collection, control mapping).

## Secondary roles (portfolio must not hurt these)
IT Auditor (junior), Security Compliance Analyst, Risk Analyst.

## Audience
Hiring managers for GRC/compliance teams. They skim for 10–30 seconds and want:
1. Do they understand frameworks beyond the acronyms?
2. Can they produce real GRC work products (policies, risk assessments, control tests)?
3. Can they communicate clearly with technical and non-technical people?
4. Will they learn fast?

## Value proposition (draft, edit in your own voice)
"Aspiring GRC analyst who turns frameworks into practical, working artifacts:
risk assessments, control mappings, and policy sets for realistic (fictional)
organizations, plus small automation tools that make compliance less manual."

## Proof we will build (evidence > claims)
- [ ] Risk assessment + risk register for a fictional company (NIST SP 800-30 style)
- [ ] Control mapping: NIST CSF 2.0 <-> ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A
- [ ] Policy set (e.g., Access Control, Incident Response, Acceptable Use) for the same fictional company
- [ ] One small technical project (e.g., script that checks configs / gathers evidence)
- [ ] 3–4 written pieces explaining the reasoning behind the above
